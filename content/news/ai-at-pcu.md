On 1 August 2025, Rector Prof. Dr. Ir. Djwantoro Hardjito, M.Eng. launched **AI@PCU — Empowering Future Leaders**: *Shaping Minds, Strengthening Character, Powered by AI.*

AI entered Petra's teaching and campus operations in 2023. The book *Towards an AI-Native Campus* documents 22 practices from lecturers and students, from wind and sunlight simulation in architecture crits to garment modelling in fashion.

The new **Artificial Intelligence** pathway under Informatics (FTI) takes its first intake in AY 2026/27, with core modules in data analytics, machine learning, NLP, deep learning, generative AI, digital leadership and technopreneurship.
::id
Pada 1 Agustus 2025, Rektor Prof. Dr. Ir. Djwantoro Hardjito, M.Eng. meluncurkan **AI@PCU — Empowering Future Leaders**: *Shaping Minds, Strengthening Character, Powered by AI.*

AI mulai masuk ke pengajaran dan operasional kampus Petra pada 2023. Buku *Towards an AI-Native Campus* mendokumentasikan 22 praktik dari dosen dan mahasiswa, mulai dari simulasi angin dan cahaya matahari di studio arsitektur hingga pemodelan busana di fashion.

Jalur **Artificial Intelligence** di bawah Informatika (FTI) menerima angkatan pertama pada TA 2026/27, dengan modul inti analitik data, machine learning, NLP, deep learning, AI generatif, kepemimpinan digital dan technopreneurship.
