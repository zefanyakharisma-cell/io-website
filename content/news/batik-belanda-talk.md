::en
On 8 October 2025, **Dr. Aniendya Christianna** of Visual Communication Design joined Dutch researcher Sabine Bolk for the session *Decolonizing Batik Belanda: Women, Memory and the Art of Shared Heritage*.

Her research reads Batik Belanda as evidence rather than ornament. The buketan bouquet carries European collecting habits into cloth; soldiers and warships hide in floral borders; colonial power was normalised in everyday dress.

The session called for museum–university partnerships, co-curated exhibitions and the digitisation of Dutch-held textile collections, and opens new MoUs in art history and design.
::id
Pada 8 Oktober 2025, **Dr. Aniendya Christianna** dari Desain Komunikasi Visual tampil bersama peneliti Belanda Sabine Bolk dalam sesi *Decolonizing Batik Belanda: Women, Memory and the Art of Shared Heritage*.

Risetnya membaca Batik Belanda sebagai bukti, bukan sekadar ornamen. Motif buketan membawa kebiasaan koleksi Eropa ke dalam kain; tentara dan kapal perang tersembunyi di bingkai bunga; kuasa kolonial dinormalisasi dalam busana sehari-hari.

Sesi ini mendorong kemitraan museum dan universitas, pameran yang dikurasi bersama serta digitalisasi koleksi tekstil di Belanda, dan membuka peluang MoU baru di bidang sejarah seni dan desain.
