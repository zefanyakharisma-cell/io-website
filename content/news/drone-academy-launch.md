Petra Christian University launched **Petra Drone Academy** on 13 July 2026, Indonesia's first credit-based academy for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV).

The one-year programme carries 20 credits across eight professional and two practical courses. Students move from civil aviation safety and flight theory, to building and maintaining a drone, to AI recognition of aerial data, and finish with supervised field flights.

## Why it matters

- Indonesia's drone market is projected to reach **USD 982.8 million by 2030**, up from 515.5 million.
- More than **15,000 drones** already work in Indonesian industry.
- One flight can map **2,000 hectares** for agriculture, power-line inspection, land surveying or logistics.

Graduates receive a UAV Application Technology certificate, an internship in Taiwan and access to the global UAV job market. The academy is open to any programme, and beyond Petra.

Partners: PT SkyWay Aerospace Technology, Chung Yuan Christian University and the Beijing Sino-Aviation Research Institute.

Universitas Kristen Petra meluncurkan **Petra Drone Academy** pada 13 Juli 2026, akademi pesawat tanpa awak (UAV) berbasis kredit pertama di Indonesia.

Program satu tahun ini berbobot 20 SKS, terdiri atas delapan mata kuliah profesional dan dua mata kuliah praktik. Mahasiswa belajar mulai dari keselamatan penerbangan sipil dan teori terbang, merakit dan merawat drone, mengenali data udara dengan AI, hingga penerbangan lapangan terbimbing.

## Mengapa penting

- Pasar drone Indonesia diproyeksikan mencapai **USD 982,8 juta pada 2030**, naik dari 515,5 juta.
- Lebih dari **15.000 drone** sudah bekerja di industri Indonesia.
- Satu penerbangan dapat memetakan **2.000 hektare** untuk pertanian, inspeksi jaringan listrik, survei lahan atau logistik.

Lulusan mendapat sertifikat UAV Application Technology, magang di Taiwan dan akses ke pasar kerja UAV global. Akademi ini terbuka untuk semua program studi, bahkan di luar Petra.

Mitra: PT SkyWay Aerospace Technology, Chung Yuan Christian University dan Beijing Sino-Aviation Research Institute.
