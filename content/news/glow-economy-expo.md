::en
On 17 October 2025, **35 MSMEs** showed their food and beverage, fashion, textile and craft products at the Glow Economy Expo in Gedung Q.

The expo was run by **SIK**, Petra's business technology incubator, chaired by Dr. Pwee Leng. Workshops were led by Dr. Kilala Tilaar of Martha Tilaar Group with two young founders, and Bank Jatim curated founders for financing.

Through its LEAP programme, SIK mentors selected businesses from bookkeeping and product development to digital marketing, legal compliance and seed funding.
::id
Pada 17 Oktober 2025, **35 UMKM** memamerkan produk makanan dan minuman, fashion, tekstil dan kerajinan di Glow Economy Expo, Gedung Q.

Expo ini diselenggarakan oleh **SIK**, inkubator bisnis teknologi Petra yang dipimpin Dr. Pwee Leng. Workshop dipandu Dr. Kilala Tilaar dari Martha Tilaar Group bersama dua pendiri muda, dan Bank Jatim mengkurasi para pendiri untuk pembiayaan.

Melalui program LEAP, SIK mendampingi usaha terpilih mulai dari pembukuan dan pengembangan produk hingga pemasaran digital, kepatuhan hukum dan pendanaan awal.
