**Maximillian Serafino Suprapto**, a student of Petra's English-taught International Program in Digital Media (IPDM), is credited as an animator with Ayena Studio on Visinema's animated feature **JUMBO**.

IPDM students train in the Digital Media Lab on 3D environments, character design, rigging and animation. Their work includes animated folklore films made with industry partners, such as *Puang Katoan*, *Pusentasi*, *Bulalo Lo Limutu* and *Tiny Ripple*.

IPDM is one of eight bachelor's programmes taught fully in English, and offers a 2+2 pathway with Dongseo University in Korea.

**Maximillian Serafino Suprapto**, mahasiswa International Program in Digital Media (IPDM) di Petra, tercatat sebagai animator bersama Ayena Studio di film animasi **JUMBO** produksi Visinema.

Mahasiswa IPDM berlatih di Digital Media Lab untuk lingkungan 3D, desain karakter, rigging dan animasi. Karya mereka mencakup film animasi cerita rakyat bersama mitra industri, seperti *Puang Katoan*, *Pusentasi*, *Bulalo Lo Limutu* dan *Tiny Ripple*.

IPDM adalah satu dari delapan program sarjana yang sepenuhnya berbahasa Inggris, dengan jalur 2+2 bersama Dongseo University di Korea.
