Petra Christian University is a funded partner in two European Union projects, together linking it with **21 partner institutions in 12 countries**.

## Erasmus+ Capacity Building in Higher Education

*Modern Academic Platform for Smart and Sustainable Cities Development in Asia and Southeast Asia Cities* brings together 8 institutions in 5 countries: the University of Jyväskylä (Finland), FH Joanneum (Austria), NIRMA University and Somaiya University (India), Prince of Songkla University and Payap University (Thailand), and Petra and Universitas Sumatera Utara (Indonesia).

## Horizon Europe, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

*Engineered Erythrocytes and Erythrocyte Extracellular Vesicles for Advanced Photodynamic and Sonodynamic Therapy* joins 14 institutions in 10 countries, from FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg and Technion to Chulalongkorn, Mahidol and the University of Mauritius.
